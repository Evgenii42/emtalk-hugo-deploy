A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Before you begin.

Can I try Bret without installing anything?

Yes. Choose Open web app above to use the browser preview. You do not need a web account.

What can I play?

Seed Keepers is about rhythm and reaction. Lantern Drift is about steering and exploration. A Place for Everything combines clues and arranging. Hidden Seasons asks you to spot changing patterns.

Can I choose how challenging a game is?

Yes. Open game settings to choose your game, goal and pace. Try levels, or keep the same difficulty with Steady pace.

Where does my web progress go?

It stays in this browser when browser storage is available. Web progress does not sync to the Android app. If saving is unavailable, Bret tells you.
