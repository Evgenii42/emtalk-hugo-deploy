Read the overview and planned tools

From an intention to a next step

Tapl is being developed to connect prepared programs with manageable actions and a weekly plan the person can review and adapt. Join a guided program through a planned catalog, code or invitation, review its suggested week, then come back to today's action and a later review.

For people, teams and program creators

Personal use centers on a clearer next action and an adjustable week. Team use would connect learning materials with program actions and analytics. Educators, trainers and other creators could bring their methodology into programs for their own clients.

What we are building for creators

The intended flow connects materials, an AI-drafted course, generated tasks, goal-specific metrics and weekly adaptation. Authors can review the structure, without manually assigning every task.

  1. 01 / YOUR MATERIAL

    Begin with what you know

    Upload your knowledge and materials as the starting point for a program, instead of rebuilding your method as a task list.

  2. 02 / A COURSE STRUCTURE

    Let AI draft the flow

    AI-assisted course creation would follow a defined program flow. The author can review the proposed structure before it is used.

  3. 03 / SMALLER ACTIONS

    Generate the tasks

    The program would generate manageable actions, so a curator does not have to manually assign every task to every participant.

  4. 04 / USEFUL SIGNALS

    See program-specific progress

    Milestones, task status and goal-specific metrics would connect analytics to what the program is actually trying to develop.

  5. 05 / THE FOLLOWING WEEK

    Review and adapt

    Use the week's activity and review to inform the next plan. Participants would still review and adjust what fits their week.

Questions about Tapl (14)
What is Tapl?

Tapl is a product in development that connects the idea of guided programs with smaller actions and a weekly plan you can review and adjust.

Can I use the app now?

This page introduces the concept through design examples. It is not a working Tapl app. No store availability or launch date is confirmed here.

How would I begin?

The planned starting point is a prepared program, joined through a catalog, access code or invitation. The program would supply smaller actions to work with.

What if the proposed week does not fit?

The intended flow lets you review the proposed tasks, move or remove them within the week, and confirm the plan before beginning.

Is this a habit tracker or a task list?

The idea centers on a guided program and an adjustable weekly plan, rather than asking you to design every next action from scratch. The design is still being refined.

Does this example save anything?

The discovery choices and sample task changes stay in this open page. They do not create an account or save a personal plan or task activity. Reloading starts the example again.

Can I share interest without an email?

Yes. An email for a reply is optional. Product updates are a separate, optional choice. Please avoid sensitive personal, health or financial information in comments.

Does sharing interest reserve access?

No. Sharing interest does not create a Tapl account, purchase a program or reserve access. Any product-update preference is separate from the feedback itself.

What will it cost?

Pricing is not confirmed on this page. We are not offering a purchase or subscription here.

Can I try something from EmTalk today?

Yes. Try the existing BRET breathing practice, explore the DM guest workspace, or read and listen to EmTalk articles using the links below. These are separate from the planned Tapl app.

Who is Tapl being developed for?

The idea includes people following a program for themselves, teams bringing training into everyday actions, and educators, trainers or other program creators guiding their own clients. These are planned uses, not services offered for purchase on this page.

Could I turn my own materials into a program?

That is part of the planned creator tools: upload knowledge and materials, use AI to draft a course following a defined flow, review its structure and generate smaller tasks. This website does not currently accept uploads or create courses.

Would a curator need to assign every task?

The intended flow includes automatic task generation from the program, reducing the need for manual assignment of every daily action. The author can review the course structure; participants would review how the proposed week fits.

What would the analytics show?

The planned focus is program progress: task status, milestones, goal-specific metrics and information for the weekly review. The numbers shown here are illustrative examples, not real participant results or guaranteed outcomes.
