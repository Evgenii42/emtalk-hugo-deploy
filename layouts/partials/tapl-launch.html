THE IDEA BEHIND TAPL

From an intention to a next step

A guided program, smaller actions, and room to change the plan. Here is the approach we are working towards.

  1. 01

    Find a program that fits

    Choose a guided program, or join one through a code or invitation.

  2. 02

    Make the week manageable

    See the smaller actions in a suggested weekly plan. Review and adjust the tasks before you begin.

  3. 03

    Review and adjust

    See your progress and review the next week with room for change.
